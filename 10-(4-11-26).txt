LPAD (LEFT SIDE PADDING):-
========================
1. LPAD FUNCTION IS USED TO PADD CHARACTER FROM A COLUMN /EXPRESSION.
2. IT ACCEPTS 3 PARAMETER 
      1ST IS A COLUMN/ EXPRESSION , 2ND IS A NO WHICH IS GREATER THEN THE 
    LENGTH OF 1STPARAMETER,
    3RD IS A PADDING CHARACTER BUT 3RD IS OPTIONAL
3.IF WE DONT TAKE 3RD PARAMETER THEN ADD SPACE IN LEFT SIDE OF A COL/EXP
 
 SELECT LPAD ('GEEK' , 6, '*') FROM DUAL;
 
 SELECT LPAD ('GEEK' , 6, '#') FROM DUAL;
